## Ingredientes

- 1 L cold water
- 20 g sake
- 1000 g clams (manila or cherrystone)

## Modo de preparo

1. To remove the grit from the clams, place them in cold water and allow them to sit undisturbed for one hour.
2. Wash the clams and scrub their exteriors to remove any residual dirt on the outside.
3. Combine 1 L cold water, sake, and cleaned clams in a pot.
4. Heat over high heat until reaching a simmer.
5. Cook the clams at a simmer for 10-15 minutes, or until the shells have opened and the clams are completely cooked.
6. Strain through a fine-mesh strainer and reserve until needed. You can then harvest the meat for alternative purposes.
