## Ingredientes

- 225 g dark Chinese soy sauce (or tamari)
- 225 g soy sauce
- 50 g mirin
- 15 g dried shiitake
- 5 g kombu
- 3 g MSG (optional)
- 10 g molasses

## Modo de preparo

1. Combine the soy sauce, mirin, dried mushrooms, and kombu in a sealed container.
2. Place in the fridge and rest at least 6 hours or up to 2 days.
3. When ready, heat the contents to 71 °C/160 °F, hold for 10 minutes.
4. Remove the kombu and discard.
5. Bring the contents to a boil.
6. Add the MSG and Molasses, whisking to dissolve.
7. Remove from heat and allow the tare to cool and steep, at least 30 minutes (though you can steep for much longer)
8. Strain out the mushrooms, pressing on the solids to extract as much as possible. Reserve the tare as needed. Hold in the fridge for up to 6 months.
