## Ingredientes

- 5 egg whites, and their shells, crushed, lightly whipped together
- 0.5 kg/1.1 lb of ground chicken (or ground pork if you'd like)
- Soup for clarifying (quantity should be at least 4 L), all fat removed

## Modo de preparo

1. Combine the egg whites and ground chicken, set aside
2. Bring the soup to a simmer
3. Add the egg white and ground chicken mixture to the soup, whisking thoroughly to allow the mixture to disperse.
4. Once dispersed, stop whisking and bring the soup back to a simmer. Allow a raft of coagulated meat and egg to form.
5. Allow the soup to cook for 15-30 minutes at a simmer, or until desired clarity is reached
6. Carefully strain the soup, ideally without disturbing the raft. Some opt to line a strainer with cheesecloth to discard the raft first, others use a ladle to scoop the soup from the top of the raft bit by bit.
