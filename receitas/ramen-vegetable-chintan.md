## Ingredientes

- 3 L water
- One onion, split and peeled
- One carrot, peeled, cut into large chunks
- 10 cloves Garlic
- 1 2-inch piece of ginger
- 10 g dried shiitake
- One bunch of green onions
- 20 g Kombu

## Modo de preparo

1. Add the water, onion, carrot, garlic, ginger, and shiitake to a pot
2. Bring up to a boil over high heat, then reduce to a simmer.
3. Cook at a simmer (around 95 °C/203 °F) for one hour
4. Add the green onion and kombu, turn off the heat, and let steep for 15 minutes.
5. Strain the soup and reserve until needed
