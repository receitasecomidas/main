## Ingredientes

- 2 lb backfat (or other fat-laden pork parts)
- 15-30 g water

## Modo de preparo

1. Preheat an oven to 121 °C/250 °F.
2. Add fatback and water to an oven-safe pan.
3. Cook in the oven for 2-3 hours, or until the fatback has turned golden brown and rendered fully.
4. Strain and reserve the fat as needed. If not using immediately, keep in the fridge for up to 6 months.
