## Ingredientes

- 300 g water
- 100 g soy sauce
- 6 g MSG
- 60 g salt
- 90 g gochujang
- 10 g fine Korean pepper powder
- 10 g garlic cloves
- 15 g Thai bird Chilis
- 50 g Mirin

## Modo de preparo

1. **Combine the above ingredients in a blender. Blend on high for 30 seconds or until smooth.**
2. **Reserve in the fridge for up to a week.**
