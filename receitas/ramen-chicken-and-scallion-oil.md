## Ingredientes

- 125 mL (approx. 0.5 cup) rendered chicken fat
- One bunch of scallion whites, trimmed of their ends and cut into 2-inch pieces
- 4 garlic cloves

## Modo de preparo

1. In a small saucepan, add the oil, scallions, and garlic cloves.
2. Heat over medium to medium-low heat, stirring occasionally, until the scallions take on a light golden hue and smell fragrant, around 15 minutes.
3. Remove from the heat, reserve in the pot, and allow it to cool.
4. Strain and transfer to a container. If not used immediately, store in the fridge.
