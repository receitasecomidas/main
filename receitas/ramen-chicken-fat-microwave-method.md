## Ingredientes

- Chicken skin

## Modo de preparo

1. In a microwave safe bowl, like pyrex, add your chicken skin.
2. Cook, uncovered, for 8 minutes at high power. You may wish to check on it to ensure it doesn't splatter.
3. Strain and reserve the skins. They won't be crispy at this stage, so to get there, pop them back into the microwave without the strained fat for a few more minutes.
4. If not using the fat immediately, keep in the fridge for up to 6 months.
