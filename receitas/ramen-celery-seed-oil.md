## Ingredientes

- 250 mL (approx. 1 cup) rendered chicken fat
- One half white onion, cut into fine dice
- 1.5 tbsp celery seeds

## Modo de preparo

1. In a small saucepan, add the fat, onion, and celery seeds
2. Heat over medium to medium low heat, stirring occasionally, until the onions take on a light golden hue, and the oil smells fragrant, around 15 minutes.
3. Remove from the heat, reserve in the pot and allow to cool.
4. Strain and transfer to a container. If not using immediately, keep in the fridge for up to 6 months.
