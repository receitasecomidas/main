## Ingredientes

- Chicken skin
- 15-30 g water

## Modo de preparo

1. Add the skin and the water to a pan and heat, over medium heat. You can omit it if you'd like the additional flavor browning provides. You can basically cook this forever as long as you replace the water, killing the heat and allowing the fat to separate after 45 minutes or so.
2. However, if you want additional browning flavor, don't add any additional water, and cook the water off and allow the skins to fry in their own fat, allowing the skins to take on a golden brown color. Cook for approximately 45 minutes.
3. Strain and reserve the crispy skins as desired.
4. If not using the oil immediately, keep in the fridge for up to 6 months.
