## Ingredientes

- 225 g soy sauce
- 50 g mirin
- 20 g brown sugar
- 225 g bacon dashi
- 40 g salt
- 5 g MSG

## Modo de preparo

1. Combine all ingredients in a pot.
2. Bring to a boil to dissolve the salt, sugar, and MSG.
3. Allow tare to cool, then use it as needed.
4. If not used immediately, store in the refrigerator for up to a month.
