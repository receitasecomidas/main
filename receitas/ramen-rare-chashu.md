## Ingredientes

- Pork coppa or pork loin
- 2% weight of the roast in salt
- 2% weight of the roast sugar
- White pepper to coat
- Granulated garlic to coat

## Modo de preparo

1. Using butcher's twine, tie the pork into a round shape.
2. Coat the pork in the salt and sugar.
3. Add white pepper and garlic powder as desired to form a full rub on the exterior. The roast is large, so you can often be aggressive with seasoning here.
4. Place the meat in a zip lock bag, and place it in the fridge for at least 24 hours. Shake the bag as necessary to promote even cure.
5. When ready to cook, preheat a water bath to 63 °C/145 °F using a sous vide water circulator, or whichever temperature you prefer.
6. Add the zip lock bag to the bath, removing air surrounding the pork. Use either a vacuum sealer or the water in the immersion circulator to push out the air before sealing the bag.
7. Cook the pork belly for 6 hours.
8. Remove the meat from the bath, place on a wire rack set in a sheet tray
9. Preheat your oven to 550 F, or as high as it will go.
10. Cook the pork for 10-30 minutes, or until the exterior is charred, browned, and sizzling.
11. Remove from the oven and allow to cool, then place in the fridge and allow to set and cool completely.
12. Use a slicer to slice thin pieces, or a very sharp knife.
