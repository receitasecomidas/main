## Ingredientes

- 230 g water
- 20 g dried scallop
- 10 g dried shrimp
- 13 g dried konbu
- 66 g sake
- 33 g mirin
- 10 g MSG
- 60 g sea salt

## Modo de preparo

1. Soak the scallop, shrimp, and konbu in the water, for at least 6 hours and up to 24, in the fridge.
2. When ready to make, add the dried products and water to a saucepot. Cook on medium heat until the cooking liquid registers at 160 F degrees. Hold here for 5 minutes.
3. Remove the kombu and bring to a boil, then down to a simmer, and cook for 30 minutes, covered.
4. In a separate pot, add the sake and mirin, boil until the smell of alcohol has evaporated, around 5-10 minutes.
5. Strain the konbu/seafood liquid into the pot with the sake. Discard the seafood.
6. Add the MSG and Salt, bring to a boil to dissolve.
7. The final yield should be 300 ml, add water back to get to this total amount.
