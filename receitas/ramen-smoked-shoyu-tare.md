## Ingredientes

- 300 g smoked soy sauce (add soy sauce to a pan, place in a smoker at 110 °C/225 °F, and smoke until reduced by half)
- 250 g smoked mushroom liquid (smoke mushrooms in a deep pan at 110 °C/225 °F, until withered)
- 50 g mirin
- 20 g brown sugar
- 15 g salt
- 20 g MSG

## Modo de preparo

1. Combine the above ingredients in a small saucepan, and bring to a boil to dissolve the salt and sugar.
2. Once fully dissolved, allow the tare to cool, then use it as needed.
