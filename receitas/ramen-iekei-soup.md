## Ingredientes

- 1 kg/2.2 lb neck bones
- 1 kg/2.2 lb femurs, split to expose the marrow
- 1 kg/2.2 lb chicken backs
- ½ Pork trotter
- 8 garlic cloves (optional)
- 6 L water

## Modo de preparo

1. Add all pork bones to a pot with fresh water. Bring to a boil, then down to a simmer, and skim the scum that rises to the top of the pot. Do this for 15-20 minutes, or until little scum is rising. The scum goes through several phases here, you'll know when the scum is pretty much done rising. This blanch is integral for a white tonkotsu, don't skip it, and don't end it prematurely.
2. Cook at a medium boil for 10 hours, stirring frequently.
3. Add the chicken, cook for an additional 6 hours.
4. If using, add aromatics to the broth in the last hour.
5. Strain the soup, reserve the soup as needed.
