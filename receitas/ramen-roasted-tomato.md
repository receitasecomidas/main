## Ingredientes

- Tomatoes (I like Roma tomatoes, but any tomato you like will work)
- Salt
- Pepper
- Olive Oil

## Modo de preparo

1. Preheat your oven to 121 °C/ 225 °F.
2. Slice the tops and bottoms off of the tomato, then slice the tomato in half lengthwise (so that you have two round, fat disks of tomato).
3. Place the tomato on a baking sheet, season with salt, pepper, and drizzle with olive oil.
4. Roast the tomatoes until they are plush, withered, and have shrunk in size, around 2-3 hours.
