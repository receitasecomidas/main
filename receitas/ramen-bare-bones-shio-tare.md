## Ingredientes

- 500 g water
- 50 g salt
- 33 g MSG

## Modo de preparo

1. Combine the above ingredients in a saucepan, and heat to dissolve the tare.
2. Cover the saucepan and allow it to cool before storing indefinitely in the fridge.
