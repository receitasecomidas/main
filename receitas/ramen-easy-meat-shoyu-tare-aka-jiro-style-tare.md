## Ingredientes

- Chashu, cooked in broth until tender (see Jiro Broth Recipe for examples)
- 400 g soy sauce
- 100 g mirin
- 15 g MSG

## Modo de preparo

1. Combine the soy sauce, mirin, and MSG in a containter
2. Place the chashu into the liquid, reserve in the fridge for 2 hours.
3. Remove the chashu, use it for eating or other purposes. Store the tare in a sealed container for up to 2 months.
